Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026  ·  Landford Auto Garage, Five Star Road, Nairobi, Kenya
Summary: We collect only what we need to serve you. We don't sell your data. We don't share it without your consent. You can request deletion at any time by contacting us on WhatsApp.

1. Who We Are

Landford Auto Garage is an automotive repair and servicing business located on Five Star Road, Nairobi, Kenya. We operate this website to provide information about our services and allow clients to book appointments.

Contact: 0706 525 927 (WhatsApp)  ·  0723 574 281

2. What Information We Collect

When you use our website or contact us, we may collect:

  • Name — when you fill in our booking form
  • Phone number — to confirm and follow up on bookings
  • Vehicle information — make, model, year to prepare for your service
  • Preferred service date — to schedule your appointment
  • Message content — any notes or descriptions you provide
  • Website usage data — page views, sections visited (stored locally in your browser, not on our servers)

We do not collect payment card details, national ID numbers, or sensitive personal data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm and manage your service booking
  • To contact you about your vehicle or appointment
  • To improve our website and services
  • To respond to enquiries sent via WhatsApp or our contact form

We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing or share it with third parties for advertising.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses browser localStorage (not traditional cookies) to store:

  • Your cookie consent preference
  • Anonymous page visit counts (no personal data)
  • Your service booking submissions (stored locally on your device)

This data stays on your device. It is not transmitted to any external server. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this website.

We use Google Fonts, loaded from fonts.googleapis.com. Google may collect anonymised request logs as part of this service.

5. WhatsApp Communication

When you click a WhatsApp button on our site, you are redirected to WhatsApp. Any conversation that takes place is subject to WhatsApp's Privacy Policy. We store WhatsApp conversations within WhatsApp only and do not export or share them.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share data in these limited cases:

  • When required by Kenyan law or court order
  • With your explicit consent

7. Data Retention

Booking information submitted through our form is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. It does not leave your device unless you explicitly share it with us via WhatsApp or phone. We retain any WhatsApp communication records for up to 12 months for service quality purposes.

8. Your Rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via WhatsApp at 0706 525 927.

9. Security

Our website implements reasonable security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption on all pages
  • Input sanitisation to prevent code injection
  • Rate limiting on form submissions to prevent abuse
  • No storage of sensitive data on external servers

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use WhatsApp for sensitive communication.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a child has submitted information via our site, please contact us immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page. We recommend reviewing it occasionally.
